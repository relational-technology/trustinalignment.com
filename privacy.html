Privacy

Last updated: 17 June 2026

Trust in Alignment exists to help people feel less alone. That care extends to your information: we collect as little as possible, and we never sell it. This page explains what we hold and why.

What we collect

When you email us at info@trustinalignment.com or book a program, we receive the details you choose to share — your name, your email, and whatever you tell us about where you are. We keep this only to stay in touch with you and to run the programs you ask for.

Payments for programs are handled by a third-party payment processor. We do not see or store your full card details.

The Seeker app

Your reflections in The Seeker are private by design. They are encrypted, and the encryption key belongs to you — they are never sold, mined, or used to train anyone else's systems. You can delete your data from within the app at any time.

This website

This site does not run advertising trackers. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of serving those files.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it — just email info@trustinalignment.com and we will take care of it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Questions are always welcome.
